# Session 2 — Method-Fit Matrix

**Question:** How should a predictive decision-support artifact, and its economic
usefulness, be developed and evaluated in one sublevel-stoping operation?

I compared three research strategies (not algorithms) using the E-D-F-C-V criteria
and the weights from class. Each score is from 1 to 5.

| Criterion | Weight | A: Design science with quantitative evaluation | B: Longitudinal quantitative case study (no recommender) | C: Quasi-experiment with operational changes using current tools |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| E: Epistemological fit | 30 | 5 | 4 | 4 |
| D: Data availability | 20 | 4 | 4 | 3 |
| F: Feasibility | 15 | 3 | 4 | 2 |
| C: Contribution type | 20 | 5 | 3 | 4 |
| V: Venue fit | 15 | 4 | 4 | 4 |
| **Weighted score (/5)** | 100 | **4.35** | **3.80** | **3.50** |

Weighted score = sum(weight × score) / 100. Gate: any score below 2 eliminates an
option; none was eliminated.

## Why these scores

**A — Design science.** It fits best (E5, C5) because my goal is to build and evaluate
a decision-support prototype. Data are available (D4), but their quality and the
permissions still need to be checked. Feasibility is the weak point (F3): building the
prototype and getting authorization takes time.

**B — Longitudinal case study.** A coherent and easier option (F4), because it only
studies associations without changing operations. But it would not produce the
artifact I want, so its contribution is lower (C3).

**C — Quasi-experiment.** Interesting for testing real design changes (C4), but it
depends on future field trials (D3) and on General Management approval, and it is
hard to control for geology, distance and fleet (F2).

## Decision

I selected **A**. Option B is still a valid fallback if field trials are not
authorized, and C could later become the evaluation phase of the prototype.
Choosing A does not mean machine learning has to beat simpler models; that is what
the evaluation will show.

Epistemological fit has the highest weight because the strategy has to match the kind
of knowledge I want to produce. With equal weights for all criteria, the order stays
the same (A 4.2, B 3.8, C 3.4).

**Open tension:** if trials are denied, I will need to reassess feasibility.

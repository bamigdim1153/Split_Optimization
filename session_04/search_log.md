The broad fallback query has low precision. Restricting it to the first 50 results and using prior candidates introduces selection and access bias. The final ten priority papers were not all identified in Semantic Scholar, as requested in slide 44.

Eligibility applied at title screening: blast-fragmentation prediction, measurement or downstream mining outcomes; empirical, modeling or methodological reports with evaluable outcomes. Underground is prioritized; caving, tunnels and surface work are explicitly complementary. Mechanical cutting, support/stability and non-blasting fragmentation were excluded. Potentially relevant titles were advanced even when no abstract was available. Full-text eligibility required a relevant evaluable outcome; seven reports could not be retrieved, not excluded as scientifically irrelevant.

The broad eligibility rules existed in review_protocol.md before this search; this execution was not prospectively registered. The source/date/type limits and fallback are documented here. Repeat searches may reorder Crossref results; use the frozen CSV for auditing this run.

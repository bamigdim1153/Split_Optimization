# Session 1 — Paradigm Justification Statement

## 1. Research topic and context

I work at an underground mine in southern Peru where production stopes are mined by
sublevel stoping. We see high explosive consumption, oversize fragments, transport costs
above the monthly budget, and missed monthly tonnage targets. I still need to verify
these observations against company records. My idea is to build a prototype that links
fragmentation prediction with an economic assessment of blast-design alternatives for
this operation.

## 2. Preliminary research question

To what extent can a fragmentation-prediction prototype support blast-design decisions
that improve the balance of drilling, explosives, loading, transport and
secondary-breaking costs in this sublevel-stoping operation?

## 3. Selected paradigm and justification

I chose design science because my main contribution is an artifact: a decision-support
prototype that I will build and evaluate. The work follows the design-science cycle of
diagnosis, requirements, development and evaluation, using the current standard design
as the reference.

A purely positivist approach does not fit as the main frame, because I want to build
and evaluate a solution, not only test associations; quantitative testing will still be
part of the evaluation. Interpretivism does not fit because my question is not about
meanings or lived experience. I did not choose mixed methods because, at this stage,
I have no qualitative question that the study needs [M1].

## 4. Data, method, validation and contribution

- **Data:** about 120 historical blasts already recorded, plus about 180 new blasts
  over at least six months, if data quality and permissions allow. Fragmentation is
  measured with Split Desktop after transport, so it describes the material at that
  stage, not at the blast face.
- **Method:** compare simple baselines and machine-learning predictors without
  assuming a winner, keeping model development separate from validation on new blasts.
- **Validation:** prediction error (MAE/RMSE) on future blasts, plus an economic
  comparison of design alternatives.
- **Contribution:** an evaluated prototype and a reproducible protocol. Whether this is
  new must be confirmed by the literature review.
- **Possible venues:** *Mining Technology* and *Mining* [R01, R03].

## 5. One doubt or tension

My main uncertainty is whether General Management will authorize design trials. I also
need permission for academic use and publication. If trials are not authorized, my
conclusions will be limited to predictions and analytical comparisons, and I will not
claim cost savings demonstrated in the field.

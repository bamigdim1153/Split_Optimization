## 1. Title

## 2. Abstract

## 3. Introduction and problem
High explosive consumption, oversize fragments, transport-budget deviations and missed monthly tonnage targets are reported, not yet quantified. The existing standard design prepared using AEGIS and technical calculations is the operational comparator.

## 4. Literature review
Systematic synthesis remains pending, consistent with the oral instruction not to develop it yet. Session 4 contains real candidate sources, not a proven research gap.

## 5. Questions, hypotheses and objectives
Main question: to what extent can the prototype support decisions that improve the cost balance? Objectives: audit data, compare predictors, validate prospectively and evaluate authorized trials. Proposed predictive hypothesis: a selected predictor reduces P80 error against baselines under an identical evaluation protocol. Economic effects require separate evaluation without promised percentages.

## 6. Methodology
Design science with quantitative evaluation; 120 historical events reported and approximately 180 future events projected. Confirm whether an event represents a ring, blast or stope; separate time/groups, fit preprocessing/tuning on training only, and evaluate new data before updating. Measure MAE/RMSE, compatible costs and production separately. An uncontrolled before/after comparison does not establish causality.

## 8. Expected outputs
An evaluated prototype, reproducible protocol and comparisons, including negative findings. No savings, ANN superiority or production improvement is guaranteed.

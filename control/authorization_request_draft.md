# Draft request to General Management — NOT SENT

Subject: Authorization for academic analysis and controlled publication of mining research information

I request authorization for a doctoral research project on fragmentation prediction and economic assessment in production stopes using sublevel stoping. The proposed scope covers historical records and at least six months of prospective observation, subject to feasibility and university requirements.

Please record separate decisions for (1) access and academic analysis of historical and future operational data, (2) use of anonymized or aggregated results in university submissions and publications, (3) any public release of data, images or code containing company information, and (4) any proposed operational trial. Permission to analyze data must not be interpreted as permission to publish it or change a blast design.

Requested data categories are event/stope IDs and dates, pre-event geology/design attributes, traceable fragmentation measurements, explosives usage, tonnage, haulage distance and compatible cost components, secondary breakage and equipment/availability context. Only approved fields will be collected; identifiers and location details will be minimized.

Company records will be stored in approved restricted locations, separate from the public GitHub repository. Public materials will use synthetic data until specific written release approval is obtained. Proposed releases will undergo company review. Operational changes will follow the mine's technical approval procedures; this request does not authorize such changes.

Decisions to record: authorized purpose, permitted fields/date range, access holders, storage/retention rules, publication-review process, restrictions, approving authority and date. Researcher identity, institutional contact and any university review reference must be supplied from genuine records before submission.

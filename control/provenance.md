# AI use declaration

I used AI tools as ChatGPT and Claude throughout this portfolio.
The AI helped me to:

- draft and organize the session documents in English;
- adapt the course notebooks (NB1, NB3, NB4, Sessions 6 and 7) to English and execute them;
- write the synthetic-data generator and the experiment scripts in `src/`;
- run the literature searches (Crossref and Semantic Scholar), screen the records,
  generate the PRISMA 2020 flow diagram with the official PRISMA2020 R package
  (run through GitHub Actions) and import the included reports into Zotero
  for Session 4.

My part: I chose the research topic, provided the mine context, wrote the Session 6
reflection, and reviewed the documents. I am responsible for the content and can
explain the methods and results.

## Mine context (my own report, not yet verified against company records)

One underground mine in southern Peru; production stopes mined by sublevel stoping;
about 120 historical blasts; about 30 new blasts per month; roughly 90% estimated
traceability; Split Desktop measurement after transport; standard design with AEGIS;
monthly budget references; no agreed acceptable-P80 criterion yet. General Management
permission is pending, so no company data are in this repository.
